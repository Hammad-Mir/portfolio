About

A researcher-engineer interested in useful visual intelligence.

I am a Computer Vision Research Engineer with an MSc in Data Science from Newcastle University. I enjoy work that requires both careful experimentation and the discipline to turn an idea into a dependable system.

How I approach the work

I am most interested in problems where a model is one part of a larger system: the data needs a better definition, evaluation needs to reflect real conditions, and the output needs to support a downstream decision. That perspective has led me from medical imaging and weak supervision to industrial document intelligence and knowledge systems.

Current focus

My current work centres on industrial computer vision and document intelligence—building pipelines that recover structured information from engineering drawings and safety documentation. I also continue to explore representation learning, vision-language models, and biomedical imaging.

Working with others

I have led technical direction for small teams of interns and junior engineers, mentored interns across multiple cohorts, and supervised an IIT Delhi final-year project that received first prize. I try to make research questions and engineering constraints visible early so people can make stronger decisions together.

Outside the work

Outside of engineering, I make time for cycling, weight training, photography, and reading. They are useful counterweights to long stretches of debugging and experimentation.
