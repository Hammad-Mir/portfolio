Research

Visual systems that work with imperfect evidence.

My research interests sit at the intersection of computer vision, representation learning, and applied AI: especially cases where visual data is noisy, complex, expensive to label, or difficult to turn into a useful decision.

MSc dissertation

Weakly supervised lung-adenocarcinoma segmentation

My dissertation investigated weakly supervised semantic segmentation for histopathology, where detailed pixel-level annotations are costly. The work used a vision transformer classifier to generate pseudo-masks from image-level labels, then trained a segmentation model with pixel-adaptive refinement.

The central question was not merely whether a model could segment tissue, but whether a useful segmentation signal could be recovered from substantially weaker supervision.
