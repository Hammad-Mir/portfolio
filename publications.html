IJARESM · Volume 9, Issue 12 · 2021

Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia Detection in Microscopic Cell Images using Deep Learning Models

Mir, H. U. E., Hussain, A., Banka, A. A., & Shah, S. Z. A.

First-author paper evaluating standard CNN architectures and a proposed 14-layer CNN on 10,661 single-cell microscopy images. The proposed model reached 91.41% accuracy; the study also documented its limited generalization across different acquisition and preprocessing standards.
